## The narrower guard

A decision of the human approved a narrower guard after the review. The guard calls `readCurrent` on the roots of `_fields` and `_values`, not on the registry object, and the move writes each root that has children. For a tree that keeps its keys in its root, the guard also reads the registry object current, and the move leaves the root unwritten, so a resolution of a conflict on the root cannot merge a racing write into the trees that the move left. A write of a record goes through the nodes of the tree, and BTrees reads them current itself; the guard is what makes a change of a field in place conflict. `plone.restapi.tests.test_addons.TestAddons.test_install_specific_profile`, which failed with the guard on the registry object, passes.
