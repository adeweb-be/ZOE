# ZOE-105: handoff

## State

`card/ZOE-105` holds the acceptance-test skeleton (`crates/zoe-server/tests/reply_memory.rs`) and a merge of `main`. No code change yet. The card stops on a question: the card was written for a session that runs one call at a time, and the pipelined calls of ZOE-055, merged since, make its invariant unreachable as written without a choice that the specification does not make.

## The conflict

The card asks that the replies that wait for one client, the frame that the writer sends included, hold at most 1 MiB beyond one frame, and that "the session runs its next call only while they hold at most 1 MiB".

On `main`, the calls of one connection run concurrently ("Connection handling" in the specification): each call takes one of 4 slots and room for its reply before it starts, and the calls that read run on the read pool. A call must not wait on a thread of the read pool to put its reply (`tests/clients_that_read_nothing.rs`), so a gate in bytes can only sit where the call takes its ticket, before it runs. The size of a reply is known only after the call ran.

So a gate "start a call only while the waiting replies hold at most 1 MiB" lets a burst through: a client sends 10 loads, the reader starts 4 of them while nothing waits yet, and each puts a reply of 4 MiB. The client then holds 4 replies, 16 MiB, not 1 MiB beyond one frame. With the 16 clients of the acceptance test that is about 256 MiB plus the buffers of the loads that run, against a limit of less than 256 MiB. The test would most likely fail. On `main` today a client holds about 5 replies (4 in the session queue and 1 at the writer).

No design that keeps the calls of one connection concurrent can know the size of a reply before the call runs. So the exact bound needs one of the choices below.

## Options

1. **Exact bound: the calls that can give a large reply run one at a time per connection.** The calls of the read pool, and `sendBlob`, take a per-connection turn as well as their slot; a call starts only when no other such call of the connection runs and the waiting replies hold at most 1 MiB. The calls of the transaction (the lane) keep running beside them, so a load still passes a waiting vote. The acceptance test stays as written and should pass with margin (about one reply per slow client). Cost: the loads of one connection no longer run concurrently with each other, against "the calls of one connection run concurrently" and "pipelined loads return out of order" in the specification. A Plone instance that shares one connection between its threads gets its loads served one at a time again, as before ZOE-055.
2. **Gate at the start only.** A call starts only while the waiting replies hold at most 1 MiB. The bound becomes "1 MiB beyond one frame for each of the 4 slots". The loads keep running concurrently. The acceptance test needs new numbers (an edit of the test, so a decision of the human), and its gap to `main` (about 4 against 5 replies per client) is small.
3. **An adaptive window per connection.** As in option 2, a call starts only while the waiting replies hold at most 1 MiB. Also, a connection starts with one call of the read pool at a time. A reply that the session queues while the waiting replies hold at most 1 MiB opens the window by one call, up to the 4 slots. A reply queued over 1 MiB closes it to one. A new or slow client holds about one reply, a fast client gets the full concurrency. The acceptance test stays as written. The bound is exact only for a client that is already slow: a fast client that stops reading can hold up to 4 replies once, until the window closes. The specification names nothing like it, so it is a new mechanism.

The download is the same in every option: `sendBlob` puts its next chunk only while the waiting replies hold at most 1 MiB, on its own thread.

## Next step

The human picks an option (or another). Then the builder implements it in `outbox.rs` (a byte count of the session queue and the writer frame, released when the writer has written the frame), `pipeline.rs` (the gate at the ticket), `blob.rs` (the chunk gate), and updates `crates/zoe-server/CLAUDE.md` and the "Wire contract" and "Connection handling" paragraphs of the specification.
