## The stop-and-ask: the time of the gate set

ADR 0037 says that ZOE-193 measures the time of the gate set against the budget of a benchmark job (about 30 minutes), and stops and asks if it is above. It is above.

The gate set ran on the development laptop (Intel Core Ultra 7 155H, CPython 3.14t), one job per cell, `just bench run --gate --storage zeo zoe-native floor --cell <cell>`:

| Cell | Job time |
| --- | --- |
| threads 1, 1 KB | 168 s |
| threads 16, 1 KB | 458 s |
| processes 16, 1 KB | 469 s |
| threads 1, 100 KB | 1034 s |
| threads 16, 100 KB | 5239 s |
| processes 16, 100 KB | 4770 s |

The two cells of 16 workers at 100 KB are each near three times the budget. The whole set took 3 h 22 min.

Possible ways:

- A. Fewer rounds in the cells of 16 workers at 100 KB (for example 3), with the verdict on the median of those rounds.
- B. Split each of those cells into one job per scenario (`--cell` and a new `--scenario`), five jobs of about 16 to 18 minutes.
- C. Fewer objects per worker in the cells of 100 KB.
- D. Accept jobs of about 90 minutes for the phase gate only.
