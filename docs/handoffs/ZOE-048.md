# ZOE-048 — handoff

## State

The branch `card/ZOE-048` holds the walking skeleton of `zoe serve`:

- `crates/zoe-server/src/serve.rs`: `zoe_server::serve::{Server, ServeOptions, Error}`. `ServeOptions::new(path)` listens on a free port of 127.0.0.1; `listen(addr)` sets the address. `Server::start` binds with `SO_REUSEADDR`, opens the store and accepts clients. `Server::stop` and the drop close every connection and the store.
- `crates/zoe-server/src/cli/serve.rs`: `zoe serve --path --listen` (twins `ZOE_PATH`, `ZOE_LISTEN`, default `127.0.0.1:8100`). It writes `listening <address>` and serves until SIGTERM or SIGINT, then exits with 0. Without the feature `serve` (the console script), it fails with 1 and names the `zoe` binary.
- `crates/zoe-server/src/protocol/`: the frame codec, the messages, the extension pickle and the session. A session runs the calls of one connection in order on one thread of the blocking pool. It serves `register`, `lastTransaction`, `get_info` (minimal), `new_oids`, `loadBefore`, `tpc_begin`, `storea`, `vote`, `tpc_finish` and `tpc_abort`.
- `python/zoedb/src/zoe/testing.py`: `ZoeServer`. It finds the binary through `ZOE_BINARY`, which `just test` sets.

Green: `just check`; the unit tests of `zoe-server`; 9 of the 10 tests of `crates/zoe-server/tests/serve.rs`; the 5 tests of `python/zoedb/tests/server/test_walking_skeleton.py` on 3.13; `test_console_serve.py`.

## What failed

`a_store_written_in_process_serves_as_it_is` asserts that the first `new_oid` of a new store is `Oid(0)`. zoe-core gives `Oid(1)`: a new store writes `next_oid = 1`, because oid 0 is the root. The question is on the card: the proposed fix of the test writes the root at `Oid(0)` without `new_oid`.
