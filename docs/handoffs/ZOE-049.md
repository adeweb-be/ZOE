## What failed, and why

The first test fails at `storage._server.get_size_info()` with `AttributeError`. Past that line, four more points stop it. "The acceptance test" at the end of the finding gives them in full:

1. `ClientStorage._server` is a `ClientThread`. It has no `get_size_info` and no `getInvalidations`.
2. The stock server does not register `get_size_info`. A call closes the connection.
3. A stock pair sends no `lastTransaction` in that flow, and `list(storage.iterator())` sends no `iterator_record_start`, `iterator_record_next` or `iterator_gc`.
4. The test does not call `tpc_abort` after the stale-serial `ConflictError`. The next `commit` waits forever on the commit lock of `ClientStorage`.
5. `storage.pack` raises `KeyError`: the store has no root object.

Each fix needs an edit of the acceptance test, or a change to stock ZEO, which the invariants forbid. The question is on the card.
