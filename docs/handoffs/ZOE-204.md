# Handoff: ZOE-204

## State

Step 1, the spike of the early-lock hook, is done and committed on `card/ZOE-204`: `docs/spikes/early-lock-hook.md`. Draft pull request #419, stacked on #418 (ZOE-203). Nothing else of the card is built.

The finding: `Connection.register` is the same in ZODB 6.2, 6.3 and 6.4, and a subclass of `Connection` sees the first change of each object, on the thread that changes it. But ZODB reads every object at the snapshot of the start of its transaction, and `register` comes after the load of the object. A writer that waits for the intent lock of another writer is then stale when the holder commits. So the design of section 9 of the specification (an intent lock in `register`, sent to the server in batches) cannot give 0 `ConflictError` on an object that cannot merge, the acceptance test of F7. A probe on `zoe.Storage` and a model of 4 writers and 20 increments show it.

## Blocked on

2. Which mechanism lets a waiter read after the grant: fail fast; fail fast and keep the lock for the retry; or lock before the read?

The others: who owns an intent lock before `tpc_begin`, the order of the locks at the vote, where a waiting `lock_intent` runs in `zoe serve`, the form of the batching, how `early-locks on` selects the class of the connection, the classes that the client skips, `native false`, and `readCurrent`.
